# Terms of use

Prepared November 8, 2026.

Matrix Mail Organizer is a private filing tool operated by Ken Schlenker for accounts whose owners have authorized access. This website does not offer public registration or a commercial service.

The organizer applies the account owner’s chosen category and sender-filing rules. Google’s categories can include important notices, receipts, and account messages. The owner remains responsible for reviewing filed mail and adjusting or disabling rules as needed.

Availability depends on Google services, valid account authorization, and the private server. Processing may be delayed or interrupted; the tool is not an emergency-notification service and no uninterrupted availability is promised.

The account owner may revoke Google authorization at any time. Already-filed mail and labels remain in the mailbox until changed. The [privacy notice](privacy.html) explains the organizer’s handling of account information.
